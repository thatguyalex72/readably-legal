Offtext

Save it once. Find it again.

Offtext keeps the articles, recipes and books you find online as clean, readable text. No ads, no clutter, and it all works with no signal.

Beta coming soon for iPhone and iPad

Lists and lists. Nothing findable.

Saved posts in one app, screenshots in another, forty open tabs and a notes file you never reopen. Offtext puts all of it in one tidy place. It keeps just the words, leaves the ads behind, and stores them on your device so they open anywhere.

How it works

Yours, and only yours

  • No account. Nothing to sign up for.
  • No ads, no tracking. The app measures nothing about you.
  • Stored on your device. With iCloud on, it syncs between your own devices.
  • Also saves your own PDFs and EPUB books, and new posts from sites you follow.
